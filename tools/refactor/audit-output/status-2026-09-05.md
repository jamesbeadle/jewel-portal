## What happened

tagging, contacts, site instructions, email comms, the drawings rename, Xero write-side and
site P&L work, labour overview changes, the tender-enquiry removal, then PR #24's transient-retry
and monitoring. None of it was refactoring, and the figures show exactly the shape you would

## Reading it

The round-18 report named round 19's targets as the pair at 428 (AiFiledDocuments,
ManualWorkOrderModal), SetXeroAllocationHandler, AiRegisterTools, the two 400+ pages and
SidebarFolders/HttpLabourStore. That list still stands, but the feature work has put three
bigger things in front of it: `RunXeroCodingSlice` (621), `XeroClient.Writes` (568) and
`AdminKpis.razor` (445), all Xero/KPI code from this week, none of it yet under a
characterisation test. If the next refactor round starts from this reading rather than the
2 Sep one, those three are the new worst-first, and the Xero pair in particular sits on the
same "it writes to Xero, test it before touching it" footing the round-18 report gave
SetXeroAllocationHandler.

The comment figure (+735) is the one to watch rather than chase: it grew across 113 more files,
which is the signature of new code rather than someone re-annotating old code, and the
round-18 pattern (section headers becoming filenames when a giant is divided) will pay a chunk
of it back as the new files above get split.

Full detail is in `tools/refactor/audit-output/audit.json` and `audit-report.md` from this run.
To adopt this as the new floor: `cp audit-output/audit.json baseline.json` — but the intent of
the gate is that the refactor rounds ratchet and feature work does not reset it, so I have left
the baseline as v19.
