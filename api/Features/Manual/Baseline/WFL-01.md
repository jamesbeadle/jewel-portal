## When

You identify something on site that is not in the schedule of works, the drawings and files, or the tender pack, but the client or design team wants done.

## How — the same day

Raise a separate email per Variation item to the Project Manager.

Email title: **[Project name] – [Variation title] – [Date]**

Email content, all in one email per item:

- Area of work or issue.
- Total m² (where applicable).
- Trades needed.
- Any strip-out or supporting works required.
- Total man-days estimate.
- Materials needed.
- Plant needed.
- Rubbish to be moved or cleared.
- Any delays this may cause to the contract works.
- Full description.
- Photos.

Always cc projects@jewelbb.co.uk.

## Approval gate

Wait for written approval before proceeding with the Variation works.
