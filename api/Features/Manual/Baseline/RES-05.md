The Company Card is only for purchases that cannot be put on a trade account — typically small ad-hoc items under £100 that cannot be delivered.

## Rules — these are also in the Agreement, clause 8

- Use the Company Card only for authorised business purchases related to the project.
- Do not share the card, the card details or the PIN with any other person, including any subcontractor or substitute.
- You are personally responsible for any unauthorised or non-business use — JBB may deduct the value, and related costs, from sums due to you.
- Sharing the card or PIN, or permitting unauthorised use, is a material breach and JBB may bring the Assignment to an end immediately.

## Before you buy

- Make sure the purchase is genuinely necessary and cannot go through a trade account.
- Make sure it is not a fraudulent or scam transaction — you are personally liable for fraud losses if reasonable care is not taken.
- If unsure, contact the office or the PM before buying.
- For anything not related to the site, contact the office before buying.
- If you lose the card, notify the office immediately.

## Sign-off threshold

- Purchases over £500 require PM sign-off in writing (Agreement clause 7.2).
- Do not split a larger purchase into several sub-£500 transactions to avoid sign-off — that is a material breach.

## Receipts via Dext — every time

Every purchase made on the Company Card has its receipt uploaded to Dext on the same day.

Dext mobile app:

1. Open the Dext mobile app (iOS or Android).
2. Take a picture of the receipt. Single Mode is one document, one page. Multiple Mode is several separate documents (up to 50), each separated into its own item. Combine Mode is one document spread over several pages, or a long receipt that will not fit one frame, combined into one item.
3. Select Costs.
4. Review — add any extra information. Some fields auto-fill and cannot be edited until processing is complete.
5. Submit.

## Ringo (parking)

- A Ringo account is set up for each Site Manager.
- Parking receipts go to Dext through the same flow.
- Use the Company Card.
