Protection and cleanliness are a core ethos of the Jewel Bespoke Build standard, reflecting the wider Jewel Enterprise values — a crystal clear approach with flawless delivery.

This is not decoration. Our work is in homes — often occupied, always valued. The site must look and feel like JBB the whole way through. The workflows link to this standard rather than restating it.

## External protection

- All external floor areas — including under skips and storage zones — protected with tarpaulins and ply (both, not either).
- All Aris fencing up with JBB branding wraps.
- Hoardings, gates and access secure.
- Skips on protection.
- Pedestrian routes clear and protected.
- External lighting where required for security and H&S.

## Internal protection

- All communal carpet floors protected.
- All tiled floors protected with Ram board and Cordex — both, always both.
- All wooden floors protected with Ram board and Cordex — both, always both.
- All non-access rooms sealed with low-tack tape; any furniture covered with plastic dust sheets.
- Any separations built as stud partitions in ply or plasterboard, caulk sealed.
- All open water and waste supplies professionally capped off.
- Internal H&S signage in place; First Aid kit visible and accessible; Accident Book accessible; Fire Safety Log accessible; Site Box including generic PPE accessible; key safe in place.

## Separation and dust exclusion

- Dust separations between works and client or occupied areas at all times.
- Stud partitions to separate works from occupied areas — boards or ply, caulk-sealed, with doors fitted to separation walls.
- Extraction units where appropriate, at the point of dust generation.
- Doors to occupied areas kept closed; brush seals on the threshold gaps.

## Daily housekeeping

- Sweep, hoover, washdown — daily.
- Pedestrian routes kept clear and clean throughout the day.
- Lunch waste (wrappers, bottles, cans, cups, cartons) bagged in rubbish bags, never left around site.
- Any food or drink left in containers emptied into the skip or removed from site, never left in bags.
- Welfare spaces kept clean throughout the day.

## Weekly housekeeping

Friday big clean at the end of the day — the full site swept, washed down, tools cleaned.

## If site labour is not available

The working Site Manager does reasonable housekeeping personally to keep the JBB standard (Agreement clause 12.2). The standard is what the client sees — never the resourcing.

## Strict prohibition on site

Urinating in buckets, bottles, containers or anywhere other than the designated toilets is a serious breach of site rules and grounds for immediate removal from site.

## Welfare

- Staff restroom, WC and welfare arrangements per project.
- Welfare unit clean and stocked.
- Drinking water available.
- Hand-washing facilities.
- Heated rest area in winter where reasonably practicable.
- Adequate lighting.

## End-of-day security

The objective is the same every day, regardless of who does it: the site is secured (Agreement clause 10.2).

- Doors and gates locked.
- Hoardings and access points closed.
- Alarms set where applicable.
- Tools, plant and materials secured or stored as above.
- No unauthorised persons on site.
- Keys, fobs and access codes secured.

The Site Manager is responsible for this outcome being achieved, personally or by arrangement with site personnel. Theft, vandalism, trespass or an attempted break-in is reported to the office immediately, and to the police where appropriate.

## Branded clothing and site identification

The JBB branded shirt or jumper is provided for site identification and brand representation — not as a uniform of employment.

- When to wear: client-facing days — client meetings, design team meetings, supplier visits, or any day a client representative is reasonably expected on site. Other days, wear the branded item or your own appropriate workwear at your discretion.
- Keep it clean, presentable and in good condition. Damaged or lost items are reported to the office and a reasonable replacement supplied. Not transferred to anyone outside the JBB engagement. Returned to the office if the Assignment ends.
- No hoodies, sportswear, tracksuits or shorts on client-facing days. Jewellery must not interfere with PPE or safety.
