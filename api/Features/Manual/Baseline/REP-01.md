## Invoice route

- Invoice cut-off: by the 24th of the month.
- Payment date: 7 days after the 24th — by the end of the same month.
- Invoices show hours actually worked and the agreed hourly rate, and go to accounts@jewelbb.co.uk.

An invoice shows: the subcontractor business name, UTR, address and contact; CIS status (Gross, 20% or 30%); the period covered; hours worked with the site or project reference; the hourly rate; VAT details, with Domestic Reverse Charge wording where you are VAT-registered (Agreement clause 5.7); bank details.

Approval flow: the Site Manager submits the invoice, the PM checks it against the site diary and hours, accounts processes it, and a CIS statement is issued each pay run.

Set-off: JBB may set off or deduct from sums due where set out in the Agreement (clause 5.5). You are given 5 working days' notice and an opportunity to respond before any deduction, other than where the law requires otherwise (clause 5.5A).
