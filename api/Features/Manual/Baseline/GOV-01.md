## Purpose of this manual

This manual exists to make your job easier. It is not here to tell you how to manage a site — you are the Site Manager and that is your judgement. It is here to give you the JBB tools, processes, standards and contacts so that every project runs to the Jewel Bespoke Build standard: a crystal clear approach with flawless delivery.

Where this manual refers to the "Site Manager", that means any Manager, Foreman or Supervisor appointed by Jewel Bespoke Build to manage a site day-to-day.

## Two documents, one job

- The **Site Manager Subcontractor Agreement (v3.3)** is the legal contract between you and Jewel Bespoke Build. It sets out the commercial terms, your status as a self-employed subcontractor, and the protections both sides have.
- This **Site Manager Manual** is the operational handbook. It sets out the JBB standards, processes and tools you use to deliver the Services to that standard.

The Manual is referenced in the Agreement (clause 21) but it is not itself a contract of employment. It contains the kind of operational rules any client in the high-end residential construction sector is entitled to expect from a competent working site manager. You decide how to deliver against them; the Manual sets out what good looks like.

## How the manual now works

The manual is maintained as a modular controlled system. Each module is owned by a named lead, reviewed separately, and published to the site team only when approved. Where a process changes, the relevant module is updated without rewriting the whole manual.

## Document hierarchy

The order of precedence is:

1. The Subcontractor Agreement.
2. Approved company policy.
3. The approved workflow module.
4. Approved forms and registers.
5. Site-issued project-specific instructions.

If anything in a module is unclear, ambiguous or contradicts the Agreement, the Agreement takes precedence. Raise it with the office or a director and the affected module is amended before it is republished.

## Module controls

Every module carries: its code, its owner, who approves it, its status (Draft, In review, Approved, Superseded), its current version, the date it was last approved, its next review date, the roles it is published to, its linked forms, its linked standards and a summary of what changed.

## Updates

The manual is updated from time to time. A material update is a new approved version of the module concerned, and it is notified to you in writing.
