## Role purpose

You are a working site manager for JBB: a working delivery lead responsible for running the project to programme, quality, safety, reporting, cleanliness and client-facing JBB standards. The role blends coordination, control, site presence, reporting and reasonable hands-on support where required to keep the programme moving.

## Delivery

- Run the project programme to time, quality and the JBB standard.
- Coordinate trades, suppliers, the design team and the client.
- Reasonable hands-on works to support delivery — assisting trades, minor works, snagging, support tasks.

## Reporting

- Daily site diary (end of the working day).
- Daily project update.
- Weekly programme update (end of Friday).
- Variation, RFI and Change Order emails — same day, a separate email per item.
- The invoice cycle (clause 5 of the Agreement).

## Standards

- JBB cleanliness, protection and security standards (module STD-01).
- Site set-up to the standard checklist (module SET-01).
- Subcontractor and supplier management on site.
- Site identification and branded clothing on client-facing days.

## Compliance

- H&S, RAMS, inductions, toolbox talks — module COM-01.
- Inductions, sign-in and sign-out and RAMS confirmation through the current induction system.
- CDM 2015 duties as a Contractor. JBB retains the Principal Contractor role; you are not the Principal Contractor.
- Equality Act 2010 (as amended by the Worker Protection Act 2023): no discrimination, harassment, bullying or victimisation on any site (Agreement clause 16.2). Take reasonable steps to protect anyone on site from harassment by any party.
- UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018: handle personal data and Company/Client Information per Agreement clause 22 (module COM-02).

## Conduct and honesty

- Diary entries, sign-in records, invoiced hours and reports to the office must be accurate. Knowingly false or misleading reports are a material breach of the Agreement (clauses 9.3 and 17.3(d)).
- **Substitution.** You may, with notice, send a suitably qualified and insured substitute to perform the Services (Agreement clause 2.3(f)). Module COM-02 has the operational steps.
- **Raising concerns, no detriment.** If you witness or experience discrimination, harassment, bullying, victimisation or a serious H&S concern, raise it directly with a director (Agreement clause 16.3). The Company treats it confidentially so far as reasonably practicable. You will not suffer any detriment for raising a genuine concern.
- **Cash and purchasing.** Trade accounts via approved suppliers, no card needed. The Company Card only for the purchases set out in module RES-05, with Dext for receipts.
