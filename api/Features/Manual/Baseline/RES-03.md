## Procedure

- Follow the JBB plant hire procedure: quotes, sign-off where over the £500 threshold (Agreement clause 7.2), and approved hire suppliers (HSS and others as agreed).
- Log all plant on site in the daily site diary.
- Inspect plant on delivery — refuse anything unsafe or not as specified.

## During the hire

- Use plant for its intended purpose only.
- Keep plant secure and protected from the weather where appropriate.
- Report damage immediately to the office.

## Off-hire

- Off-hire as soon as the plant is no longer needed — avoidable hire costs are recoverable from the Site Manager (Agreement clauses 5.5 and 13).
- Plant returns are not a reason to leave site early. Where plant cannot be collected by the supplier and must be returned, plan the return around the programme so it does not compromise site delivery. The same exception as collections in module RES-01 applies.
