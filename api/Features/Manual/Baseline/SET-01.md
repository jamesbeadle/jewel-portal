## H&S Officer supplies, Site Manager confirms on site

These items are supplied or arranged by the H&S Officer. The Site Manager remains responsible for confirming each one is physically on site, displayed where required and accessible before works commence. If anything is missing or out of date, raise it with the H&S Officer immediately — works do not start until all items are in place.

- Accident Book.
- Fire Safety Log.
- Induction QR code signage in place.
- Employer's Liability Insurance certificate displayed.
- Construction Phase Plan in place.
- F10 notification submitted (where applicable).
- Duty Holder details displayed.
- Fire and Emergency Evacuation Plan displayed.
- Site Emergency Contact Sheet displayed.
- COSHH folder and Safety Data Sheets available on site.
- Site Safety Rules displayed.
- First Aider and Fire Marshal details displayed.
- Inspection records file available (scaffold, ladder, plant, fire points).
- Accident, incident and near-miss reporting forms available.
- First Aid kits.
- Fire extinguishers, alarm and fire signs.

Carpet protector, Cordex, Ram board, plastic dust sheets for loose furniture, masking tape, duct tape (including low-tack for non-access rooms), locks, padlocks and key safe, fencing and barriers, stud walls (timber, boards, screws, doors) for area divides and fixed-furniture protection, extraction units, tarpaulins and ply for driveways, paths, removable door thresholds and the skip area, skips, and the welfare unit.
