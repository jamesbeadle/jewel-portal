This applies only where JBB has provided a Company van for the Assignment. You are a representative of Jewel Bespoke Build whenever the van is being driven.

## Standards

- Keep the van clean — washed regularly, no visible rubbish on display.
- Report any damage or problems immediately to the office: 0208 109 1015 (Agreement clause 14.1).
- No phone use, texting or smoking while driving the vehicle (Agreement clause 14.1).
- Anti-theft devices (a steering wheel bar on tippers, for example) are used when locking (Agreement clause 14.1).
- Traffic fines caused by you are your personal liability (Agreement clause 14.1).
- Obey traffic laws and drive courteously.

## Weekly vehicle checks

- Petrol or diesel level — never run near empty.
- Tyre pressures.
- Oil level.
- Screenwash level.
- Coolant level.
- AdBlue level (where applicable).
- Step ladder in the van.
- Hoover in the van.

## Recovery of costs

Where the Site Manager has not maintained the van to the standards above, JBB may deduct the reasonable cost of rectification from any payment due (Agreement clauses 14 and 5.5).
