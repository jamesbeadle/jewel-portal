Before mobilising on a new project, work through this with the Project Manager and tick off each item.

**Trigger:** the project is awarded and the mobilisation date is approaching.
**Owner:** the Project Manager, with the Site Manager the action owner for readiness tasks.
**Inputs:** the programme, the drawings, the RAMS, the cost code, the supplier list.
**Output:** a signed-off mobilisation-ready status.
**Evidence:** the completed checklist below and written confirmation to the Project Manager.

## Office and project information

- Initial pre-start meeting in the office attended.
- Project programme reviewed and agreed.
- Drawings and schedule of works reviewed and understood.
- Method statements and RAMS reviewed with the H&S Officer.
- Any queries raised back to the PM in writing.
- Long-lead procurement items identified and booked.
- Cost codes confirmed.
- Subcontractor contact list collected with booking and quote confirmations.
- Own Public Liability insurance of at least £2,000,000 in date — certificate held and available on request (Agreement clause 19.1).
- CIS registration, UTR and HMRC status current — any change in CIS status notified to JBB (Agreement clause 2.5).

## Site mobilisation

- Materials and plant required for site set-up confirmed.
- Access arrangements (parking, deliveries, neighbour access) confirmed.
- Welfare arrangements confirmed.
- Skips booked or arranged.
- Building Control booked in for the relevant phases.
- Neighbour letters drafted, printed and hand-delivered.

## Compliance and pre-mobilisation

The "in the bag, in the diary, in the system" checklist, prepared before mobilising. It sits alongside module SET-01, which covers what must be physically in place on site.

Forms and registers ready (taken to site):
- Visitor Register.
- PPE Issue Register.
- RAMS Confirmation forms.
- Site Safety Checklist printed.
- Site File set up (digital and hard copy).
- Drawings, schedule and RAMS printed where hard copies are needed on site.

Admin and accounts ready:
- Subcontractor and supplier contact list collated.
- Trade account references confirmed for the suppliers in use.
- Cost code confirmed with the PM.
- Company Card on hand, where the Site Manager holds one.
- Ringo parking account confirmed active.
- Dext mobile app logged in.
- Office notified of the mobilisation date and the out-of-hours contact.

Send a written confirmation to the PM once the above is complete, before the first day on site.
