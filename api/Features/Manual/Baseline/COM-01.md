## Site Manager H&S actions and records

The Site Manager is responsible for ensuring the site H&S controls are implemented, checked, recorded and escalated in line with the Construction Phase Plan, the RAMS, the site rules and JBB procedures. The frequencies below set the minimum routine requirements; additional checks are required where risk, work activity, incidents, weather, deliveries, temporary works or interface issues make this necessary.

This module overlays the daily walkthrough in RUN-01, the weekly routine in RUN-02, cleanliness and protection in STD-01, and end-of-day security in STD-01.

### Every day

- Complete the daily site safety inspection before or at the start of works: access and egress, housekeeping, lighting, signage, barriers and site security.
- Check welfare, drinking water, toilets, washing facilities and general cleanliness are suitable and maintained.
- Confirm emergency arrangements are in place and visible: fire points, extinguishers, escape routes, first aid provision and emergency contact details.
- Check PPE standards are being met and that task-specific PPE is available, worn correctly and replaced where damaged.
- Verify that all operatives and visitors have been signed in, inducted as required, and are authorised to be on site.
- Review the day's planned activities against current RAMS, permits, the sequence of works and interfaces with other trades.
- Brief operatives where required on changes to risk, work method, access, plant movement, deliveries, weather or exclusion zones.
- Check high-risk works in progress are properly controlled: work at height, scaffolds, ladders, lifting operations, excavations, hot works, live services and confined spaces where applicable.
- Inspect plant, tools and work equipment in use for obvious defects, safe condition and correct operator use; remove unsafe equipment from service.
- Supervise works to ensure controls are being followed in practice and intervene immediately where unsafe acts or conditions are identified.
- Record and report accidents, incidents, near misses, unsafe conditions and breaches; start corrective actions and report to the H&S Officer without delay.

## Framework groups — H&S Officer to populate

The Site Manager applies these on site; the H&S Officer owns the framework, the forms and the periodic review.

**Group 1 — Policy and framework.** H&S policy statement and site-specific responsibilities. Site management structure and dutyholder chart, consistent with Agreement clause 3.3(b): JBB retains the Principal Contractor and Principal Designer roles under CDM 2015; the Site Manager operates as a Contractor. Construction Phase Plan — interface, document control and review. Worker consultation and communication arrangements.

**Group 2 — Risk and method.** Risk assessment and RAMS review procedure, including review triggers when work, sequence or personnel change, or incidents occur. Permit-to-work arrangements where required.

**Group 3 — High-risk activity controls.** Working at height and scaffolding. Lifting operations. Excavation and temporary works. Live services. Hot works. Confined spaces.

**Group 4 — Site environment and welfare.** Welfare standards and housekeeping (module STD-01). Public protection, site security and unauthorised access controls (module STD-01). Occupational health controls: HAVS, dust, noise, face-fit testing, manual handling.

**Group 5 — People, competence, monitoring.** Training and competence checks — CSCS, cards, tickets, supervision arrangements, and how they map onto the induction system and RAMS confirmation. Subcontractor pre-start and ongoing monitoring. The daily, weekly and formal inspection regime: scaffold, ladder, plant, fire point, housekeeping.

**Group 6 — Incident and assurance.** Accident, incident and near-miss reporting. RIDDOR assessment and reporting responsibility. Incident investigation and close-out of corrective actions. Monitoring, audit and periodic review of the H&S system.
