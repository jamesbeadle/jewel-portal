## When

You have a question about the works that is not answered by the schedule of works, the drawings and files, or the tender pack.

## How — the same day

Raise a separate email per RFI to the Project Manager. State who the question is for: the architect, the structural engineer, the client or the office.

Email title: **[Project name] – [RFI title] – [Date]**

Email content:

- Who the RFI is for (architect, structural engineer, client, office).
- Area of work or issue.
- Total m² (where applicable).
- Trades needed.
- Total man-days impact.
- Materials needed.
- Plant needed.
- Rubbish to be moved or cleared.
- Any delays this may cause to the contract works.
- Full description.
- Photos.

Always cc projects@jewelbb.co.uk.
