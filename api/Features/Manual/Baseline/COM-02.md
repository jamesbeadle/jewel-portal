## Substitutes — Agreement clause 2.3(f)

The Subcontractor Agreement gives the Site Manager the right to send a suitably qualified, experienced and insured substitute to perform the Services, with notice to the Company. This is an IR35-important right; the steps below make it workable on site.

### Before sending a substitute

- Notify JBB in writing (email or message to the PM and a director).
- Confirm the substitute meets the contract criteria. JBB may refuse only on objective, verifiable grounds: a valid CSCS card appropriate to the role; a current SMSTS (or equivalent) qualification; public liability insurance of at least £2,000,000 in force; the right to work in the UK; not previously banned or removed from a JBB site for breach, dishonesty or H&S grounds.
- Provide the JBB office with copies or references of the above.

### On site — the substitute's first day

- Site induction through the current induction process before any work.
- PPE and, on client-facing days, branded clothing provided or arranged.
- Substitute added to sign-in and sign-out and to the Visitor and Induction Registers.
- Substitute briefed on JBB protection, security and reporting standards (this manual).
- H&S Officer and RAMS confirmation completed for the tasks the substitute will undertake.

### Commercial position

- JBB pays the Subcontractor, not the substitute. There is no contractual or financial relationship between JBB and the substitute (Agreement clause 2.3(f)).
- Handover or training of a substitute is not paid by JBB — it is for the Subcontractor's account.
- All confidentiality, Company Card, data protection and conduct obligations apply equally to the substitute while they perform the Services. The Subcontractor remains responsible for the substitute's compliance.

## Data protection and Company/Client Information — Agreement clause 22

In providing the Services you handle personal data and other confidential information belonging to JBB and our clients ("Company/Client Information"). The Agreement sets out the legal framework; this is the practical handling guide.

### Use

Only use Company/Client Information for the purpose of providing the Services — never for personal purposes, another client of yours, or any third party (clause 22.2).

### Storage and devices (clause 22.3)

- Keep Company/Client Information secure on any device, paper file, drawing, USB stick, cloud account or messaging app used for the Services.
- Use reasonable security on any device that accesses it: PIN or biometric lock, an up-to-date operating system, no shared or public devices.
- Do not photograph, copy, forward or upload Company/Client Information to any personal account, personal cloud or third-party platform other than as required to deliver the Services.

### Mandatory reporting — immediate, within 24 hours of becoming aware (clause 22.4)

Report to a director immediately: an actual or suspected data breach (unauthorised access, accidental disclosure, hacking, phishing, lost credentials); the loss, theft or damage of any document, drawing, device, USB stick or paper file holding Company/Client Information; misuse or suspected misuse by you, a substitute or a third party; any request from a third party (a client's family, friend, neighbour, contractor or the media) for Company/Client Information; any subject access request, data protection complaint or regulatory enquiry received in connection with the Services.

### Processor obligations — Article 28 UK GDPR (clause 22.1A)

When you process personal data on behalf of JBB you act as a processor and JBB is the controller. In practice: only process personal data on JBB's documented instructions; anyone you allow to see that data is under a duty of confidentiality; apply appropriate technical and organisational measures; do not engage a sub-processor (a third-party tool, an off-shore service, a personal cloud) for JBB data without JBB's prior written consent; help JBB respond to data-subject requests, breach reporting and ICO-related obligations when asked; make information available to JBB on request to demonstrate compliance, and allow audits on reasonable notice.

### On the ending of an Assignment (clause 22.5)

- Return all Company/Client Information held in physical form.
- Permanently delete all Company/Client Information held electronically on any personal device, account, cloud, messaging app or email account.
- Confirm in writing within 7 days that you have done so.

Failure to follow this module is a material breach (clause 22.6) and may give rise to indemnity claims under clause 19.
