The weekly and monthly routines are cyclical, so they sit together here rather than as one-off workflows.

## Friday close-out

- Big site clean at the end of the day — all areas swept, washed down, tools cleaned.
- Weekly programme update submitted to the PM by the end of the working day.
- Two-week look-ahead — deliveries, sequencing, trade starts, inspections, plant required.
- Site walkround report including H&S observations.
- Plant review — anything not needed is off-hired.
- Attendance record submitted (below).

## Weekly project meeting

- Attendance.
- Programme position update.
- Open Variations, RFIs and Change Orders reviewed.
- Risks and blockers raised.

## Weekly H&S controls

- Complete and file the formal weekly site safety inspection and checklist, and track the close-out of any actions raised.
- Check that scaffold inspections, plant checks, fire point checks and housekeeping inspections are current and recorded.
- Review RAMS and permits for upcoming work and confirm revisions are issued where scope, sequence, personnel or conditions have changed.
- Review subcontractor performance, supervision standards and operative competence records, including cards, tickets and any gaps requiring action.
- Deliver or arrange the scheduled toolbox talk, record attendance, and brief any key lessons learned or recurring issues.
- Review housekeeping, waste management, storage, segregation and public protection arrangements across the whole site.
- Confirm defects, hazards and previous inspection actions have been closed out, escalated, or given target dates and owners.

## Monthly H&S review

- Carry out a monthly management review of site H&S performance with the H&S Officer or management representative: trends, recurring issues, overdue actions.
- Review accident, incident, near-miss and unsafe condition records to identify root causes, corrective actions and whether any matter may be RIDDOR reportable.
- Review whether risk assessments, method statements, the Construction Phase Plan interface, emergency arrangements and monitoring controls remain suitable and up to date.
- Check planned inspections, maintenance, testing and statutory examinations are in date for plant, lifting equipment, temporary works interfaces, fire equipment and other safety-critical controls where applicable.
- Review occupational health controls relevant to the works: dust, noise, HAVS, manual handling, face-fit, exposure monitoring and health surveillance triggers where applicable.
- Review training, induction, toolbox talk completion and competence gaps, then arrange refresher actions where required.
- Submit or update the monthly H&S summary required by JBB, where established: inspections completed, issues raised, actions closed, and the priority risks for the next period.

## Attendance record

A weekly attendance record is required each Friday for H&S records, submitted to the attendance route in module REP-01.

For half-days at two sites: Monday — Point West / 124 Chiltern Court.
