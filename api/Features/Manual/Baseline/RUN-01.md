The day's outcome, not the clock, is what matters. The Subcontractor Agreement is clear: you arrange your own attendance to deliver the programme (clause 4.1). The list below is what good looks like on a JBB site.

The daily walkround set out here is recorded on the daily site H&S inspection form per module COM-01. COM-01 is the formal H&S framework — daily, weekly and monthly actions and records. This module is the operational walkthrough; together they cover what is done on the day and what is filed against it.

## Start of day

- Arrive in good time to deliver the programme and the day's deliveries, inspections and trade starts.
- Correct PPE worn (module COM-01).
- Branded JBB shirt or jumper on client-facing days — client meetings, design team meetings, supplier visits, or any day a client representative is reasonably expected on site (Agreement clause 15.2). Otherwise wear what you choose, appropriate to the work.
- Sign in through the current induction system.

## Through the day

- Site induction for any new attendee — recorded on the Induction Register.
- Sign-in and sign-out record maintained for all subcontractors and labour.
- Visitor Register completed for any visitor.
- Quality walkround of works in progress against drawings and specification.
- Hands-on works supporting trades where the programme requires.
- Photos throughout the day: progress, issues, deliveries, plant on site.
- Daily housekeeping — sweep, hoover, washdown — with client areas protected at all times (module STD-01).
- Material deliveries and waste clearances handled.
- Plant on site logged in the diary.
- Any Variation, RFI or Change Order identified — raised today, before leaving site (modules WFL-01 to WFL-03).

## Daily reporting

Send a short daily update to the Company project group covering the site name, trades on site, key works completed, key works planned tomorrow, issues and blockers, and a photo.

The daily site diary covers: trades on site (names, companies); hours worked by each trade; works completed; programme position against plan; deliveries received; plant on site and any planned off-hires; issues, near-misses and accidents; photos of progress; subcontractor timekeeping observations.

## End of day

- Site security outcome achieved — doors and gates locked, hoardings and access closed, alarms set where applicable, tools, plant and materials secured per module STD-01. You are responsible for the outcome, whether you do it personally or by arrangement with site personnel (Agreement clause 10.2).
- Daily site diary submitted by the end of the working day.
- Sign out through the current induction system.
- No discussion of commercial or financial information with subcontractors, suppliers, clients or third parties (Agreement clause 6).

## What not to do

Items marked material breach are grounds for immediate removal from site and termination of the Assignment (Agreement clause 17).

- Material collections during the working day (module RES-01 — exceptions only).
- Plant returns during the working day (module RES-03 — exceptions only).
- Leaving site early to make a collection or return.
- Leaving site early without prior written agreement with the office — agreed in advance with the PM or a director, not "telling the group on the way out".
- Sending operatives off site to collect materials without consent when those materials should have been booked for delivery. Procurement is a planning task, not an in-day errand.
- Deviating from designed and detailed works on your own initiative. Any departure from the drawings, schedule or RAMS is raised as an RFI or Variation first. Build it differently and we own the cost and the rectification.
- Splitting purchases across several sub-£500 transactions to avoid the PM sign-off threshold (Agreement clause 7.2). Material breach.
- Sharing the Company Card, its details or its PIN with anyone, including any subcontractor, operative or substitute (Agreement clause 8). Material breach.
- Discussing the company's finances, commercial position or margins with subcontractors, suppliers, operatives or any third party (Agreement clause 6). Material breach.
- Invoicing for hours not worked. Hours on the invoice match hours actually delivered on site, as recorded in the site diary. A fundamental term of the engagement.
- Misrepresenting the position to the office — on programme, attendance, deliveries, RAMS compliance or any other reportable matter. Honesty is the engagement; being asked and not coming forward is a separate breach.
- Permitting unauthorised persons on site, including friends, family or anyone not inducted and signed in (module STD-01, Agreement clause 10).
- Using site labour, plant, materials or vehicles for non-JBB work or personal purposes.
- Any discrimination, harassment (including third-party harassment), bullying or victimisation, on site or in any communication relating to the Services (Agreement clause 16.2). Material breach.
