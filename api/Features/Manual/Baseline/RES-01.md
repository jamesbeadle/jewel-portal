## Planning

- Plan materials a minimum of one week ahead of delivery using the schedule of works and the drawings.
- Long-lead items are booked months in advance. Identify them at pre-start (module MOB-01).
- No ad-hoc buying.

## Ordering route — trade accounts

JBB has trade accounts with its approved suppliers. Material orders go through these accounts — no card needed. The Company Card (module RES-05) is only for what is set out there.

1. Identify the materials needed for the week ahead.
2. Get a quote PDF emailed from the supplier.
3. Forward the quote to the JBB Materials group as **[PROJECT & TASK]**.
4. Confirm with the PM, then place the order with the supplier on the trade account.
5. Always request the first drop or a morning slot wherever possible.
6. Confirm labour is on site to take the delivery.
7. Make sure protection is down before the delivery (module STD-01).

## Collections — discouraged

Our preference is deliveries to site. Collections cause time loss and disruption to the programme.

If a collection is genuinely necessary:

- Complete it before site start wherever possible, so it does not displace site management time during the day.
- A collection during the working day is treated as a planning failure and is not acceptable other than in the exception below.
- Exception: a collection during the day is only acceptable where a planned delivery has been delayed, postponed or failed, and the collection is necessary to keep the programme on track. Notify the PM in advance and record the reason in the daily site diary.
- Leaving site early to make a collection is not acceptable.
