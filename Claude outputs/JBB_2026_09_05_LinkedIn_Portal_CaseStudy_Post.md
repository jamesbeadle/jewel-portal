This week our project system noticed one of its own rules wasn't working, worked out what it should have said, and rewrote it. Then carried on.

That is where Jewel Bespoke Build has got to with automation, and I want to be specific, because "we use AI" has stopped meaning anything.

The portal that runs the business is now a connector inside Claude. It isn't a chatbot sat next to the data — it is the data, and the actions. Ask where an RFI stands and it reads the correspondence and tells you. Tell it to raise the variation and it raises one, as you, logged under your name.

The rules it works to are ours — how a variation is priced, what a valuation must never miss, when a subcontractor gets chased. When something goes wrong, the AI notices, works out what the rule should say, and edits it. Every edit logged. The judgement gets sharper on its own instead of going stale on someone's laptop.

Around it: every email into the shared mailbox is triaged into the record it is actually about. The accounts system is read straight into the cashflow, nothing re-keyed. The valuation report falls out of the data — not long ago it was five versions of a spreadsheet.

All on Microsoft Azure, set up properly: geo-redundant backups, thirty-five days of point-in-time recovery, monitoring that tells us before a client would.

Built with a small automation practice I've been quietly involved in. More on that soon.
