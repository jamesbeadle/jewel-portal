Every view on the rail, and everything behind it

The side nav is a single catalog — Home, the project picker, ten collapsible folders, then four folderless rows at the foot. Rows marked project resolve their {project} template against whatever the picker has selected; the rest are company-wide. Read from SidebarFolders.cs, DesktopNavigation.cs and every @page route in jpms/Pages.

Nav clamp

Since the 2026-08-11 decision every row below is gated to Managing Director and Finance Director, with administrators bypassing the gate and seeing everything. Weekly Cashflow adds Accounts — the one deliberate exception. The Admin folder's first four rows are administrators only. Every other role signs in and sees Home alone; API permissions are untouched by the clamp, so their pages remain reachable by URL.

Folderless rows, at the foot of the rail

  • Control Centre company
    /control-centre

    The mailbox intake queue and router for all correspondence across every project. Legacy slug /requests/triage lands here.

  • Document Triage company
    /document-triage

    The attachment queue the Control Centre feeds — filed out to Drawings, Payment Certificates or subcontractor records. Legacy slug /document-control.

  • Xero Cost Allocation company
    /finance/allocation

    Distributing allocated purchase lines to cost centres. Standing work, so it left the Xero folder in August.

  • Valuation Reports project
    /projects/{id}/valuation

    The picked project's live valuation report — the flagship output, elevated out of its folder to the top level.

Orphans — removed 3 September 2026

  • Estimating queue · Nurture · Sales analytics
    /estimating-queue · /nurture · /sales-analytics

    The CRM front end: three routed pages with no rail row and no inbound link anywhere in the app. Deleted along with their plumbing — LeadsTable, LeadsBySourceTable, LeadStageBadge, ILeadStore/HttpLeadStore, Features/Leads, the DI and route registrations, and their three AI page guides. 648 lines.

    Kept: the Leads API (api/Features/Leads, ~20 endpoints) and the list_leads connector tool. The pipeline has no front door now — that is a decision to revisit, not an accident.
